# RetailPulse Data Layers

## Bronze

Bronze contains raw or minimally transformed source data.

Its main purpose is preserving source information.

## Silver

Silver contains cleaned, standardized and deduplicated
data.

CDC operations are resolved into usable current-state
datasets where appropriate.

## Gold

Gold contains business-oriented aggregates and analytical
datasets.

Examples include customer 360, order summaries and product
performance.

## Analytics

Analytics models are optimized for reporting, dashboards
and machine-learning feature generation.
