# Customer 360

Customer 360 creates a consolidated representation of a
customer.

It combines information from multiple customer-related
sources.

These can include:

customer profile
orders
payments
website activity
support activity
marketing activity

Customer 360 can contain metrics such as total orders,
total revenue, average order value, recent activity and
customer engagement.

The Customer 360 dataset is used by analytics,
machine-learning systems and the RetailPulse API.
